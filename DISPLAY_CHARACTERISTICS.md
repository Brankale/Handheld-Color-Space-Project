## Terminology

- **Subpixel layout**: arrangement of the red, green, and blue subpixels, such as RGB stripe or BGR stripe.
- **Pixel pitch**: centre-to-centre distance between adjacent pixels.
- **Pixel gap**: edge-to-edge distance between adjacent pixels.
- **Intra-pixel subpixel gap**: edge-to-edge distance between two adjacent subpixels belonging to the same logical pixel, such as the gap from the red subpixel to the green subpixel or from the green subpixel to the blue subpixel. It excludes the gap between subpixels belonging to different pixels.
